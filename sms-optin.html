How to sign up for text messages

Generation Cotton LLC, doing business as Legendary Vero, texts customers only after they ask for texts. Here is exactly how a customer signs up.

1. On our website quote form

On legendaryvero.com/quote.html (Spanish: /es/quote.html), linked as "Quote request form" in the footer of every page, the customer fills in their name, mobile phone and what they want a quote on. Below the form is a checkbox. It is not pre-checked and is optional: the form can be sent without it. Next to the checkbox the customer sees:

"Yes, text me about my quote and order from Legendary Vero. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. See our Privacy Policy and Text Message Terms."

Only customers who check the box are added to text messages. We save the date, time and exact wording they agreed to.

2. By texting us first

A customer who texts our business number (772) 494-2734 first gets replies only about that conversation. Our first reply confirms the program name, says message frequency varies, and that they can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

What we send, and how to stop

Quote replies, proof and order status updates, pickup notices, and site-visit appointment confirmations. No marketing. Reply STOP to any message to stop; reply HELP or call (772) 257-6109 for help. Mobile numbers and consent are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates. See our Privacy Policy and Text Message Terms.
